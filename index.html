The Roadmap

Seamless Execution Process

How Gaze Interiors turns your vision into a turnkey dream home without stress or unexpected cost escalations.

01

Discovery & Site Laser Survey

We meet at our Bavdhan studio or your property to map your lifestyle needs, aesthetic preferences, and take exact digital laser measurements.

02

3D Visuals & Material Moodboards

Review detailed photorealistic 3D perspectives, test lighting moods, and touch physical laminates, veneers, fabrics, and stone samples.

03

Precision Factory Fabrication

Woodwork and modular units are pre-cut with high-precision CNC machinery off-site, reducing noise, dust, and on-site delays dramatically.

04

146-Point Audit & Handover

Rigorous quality inspection of hardware, electricals, and finishes followed by deep cleaning and formal handover with your 10-year warranty certificate.
